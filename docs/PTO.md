# Professional tryouts (v39, hs/game/pto.py)

From the arbitration phase until opening night you can bring up to **three** unsigned free agents to training camp
on a professional tryout (PTO).

* A tryout has no contract: it costs nothing against the cap or the 50-contract limit.
* While he is at your camp, no other club can sign him (he leaves the free-agent pool).
* On opening day the head coach writes about each one: where he would slot on your depth chart at his position (and
  whether he would play every night, be a depth option, or sit), his agent's one-year ask, and three buttons: sign
  him (one year at the ask), negotiate (his page), or let him go.
* If you neither sign nor release him, he becomes a free agent again a week after opening night.

Where: a free agent's page ("Invite to camp (tryout)") in the window; the console: `pto <player>`, `pto release
<player>`, `pto` (who is in camp).

Not yet: tryout players do not dress for pre-season games, and AI clubs do not use tryouts.
