Not legal advice; summarised from public articles, not the agreement. Status: **V** = stated by two or more
sources, **1** = one source, **?** = from the user's pasted text or older CBA, not confirmed for the new one.

## Salary arbitration
From PuckPedia's arbitration page (rules as of the prior CBA; it does not mention the 2026 CBA, so treat as **?**):
only RFAs; player elects by 5 July; club may elect (max 2 a year, once per player career) and then the award must be
at least 85% of last season's pay if he earned $2.38M+; awards are 1 or 2 years, term chosen by the side that did
not elect, 1 year only if he is one year from UFA; club may walk away from a player-elected award of $4.74M or more
and he becomes a UFA. Eligibility uses a table of age at first contract vs pro years (18-20: 4, 21: 3, 22-23: 2,
24+: 1 in the user's notes). As far as we know the NHL arbitrator may award any figure (not MLB-style pick-one-side).

## Trades
Retained salary: double retention is banned for 75 regular-season games after a trade (V). The 50% limit and the
3-slot limit are old-CBA rules, unconfirmed here (?).

## Other changes that touch the engine
- Regular season 84 games, divisional rivals four times (V). `hs/leaguedata/nhl_2025_26.py` is the 82-game structure;
  a 2026-27 structure needs the real opponent-count split, which was not found.
- Preseason 4 games; every team must carry an emergency goalie (EBUG), not counted against the cap (V).
- Recalls after the deadline: 5 allowed (up from 4) (1).

## Where the pasted text and the sources disagree
- Qualifying offers: pasted text says $660K / $1M; sources say $1.25M / $1.75M.
- The pasted text omits the 84-game season, the 60% bonus cap and the 20% / 71% salary-shape limits.
- Prospect rights "until age 22" is not what the articles describe.
